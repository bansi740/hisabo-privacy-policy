Hisabo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Please read this policy carefully to understand how information is handled when using Hisabo.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Hisabo. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Hisabo handles your information when you use our personal finance management app.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Hisabo may handle:

  • Account information such as your name and email address.
  • Financial records such as income, expenses, transfers, accounts and budgets that you enter.
  • App preferences such as your selected theme and language.
  • Information required to authenticate your account.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

Hisabo uses local database storage (SQLite) to maintain your app records on your device.

If you sign in and use cloud synchronization, relevant account and app data may also be stored in Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Cloud synchronization requires an internet connection and depends on the availability of the service.

4. How We Use Information

Information handled by Hisabo is used to:

  • Provide account registration and authentication.
  • Save and display your financial records.
  • Organize income, expenses, accounts and budgets.
  • Provide financial summaries and insights.
  • Remember your app preferences.
  • Support cloud backup and synchronization where enabled.

5. Financial Information

Financial information you enter into Hisabo is used to provide the app features you request.

Hisabo is a personal finance tracking tool. Do not enter banking passwords, ATM PINs, OTPs or other confidential authentication credentials into financial record fields.

6. Third-Party Services

Hisabo uses Firebase services for account authentication and cloud data functionality.

These services are provided by Google and are subject to their own privacy and security practices.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no electronic storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should keep your device and account credentials secure and avoid sharing access to your account.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored information may remain on your device until it is deleted or the app data is cleared.

Information synchronized to Firebase may remain in cloud storage until it is deleted through the applicable account or data deletion process.

Removing the app from your device does not necessarily delete information already stored in the cloud.

9. Your Choices

You can manage your app preferences, including theme and language, through Settings.

You should review your account and data management options before removing your account or clearing app data.

10. Children's Privacy

Hisabo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us through the available support channel.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, services or applicable requirements change.

The latest version should be made available within the app. Please review this page periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact us through the Help & Support section in Hisabo.
